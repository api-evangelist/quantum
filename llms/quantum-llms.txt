> publishers and advertisers with data and technology. Its Quantum One omnichannel platform

> buying, e-sampling, on-ground activations, influencer marketing, and out-of-home media,

## What Quantum sells

- Quantum One — omnichannel platform connecting online and offline shopping experiences
- Quantum Ads Marketplace — retailers, brands and agencies trade and exchange media across the open commerce ecosystem
- Programmatic ads, in-app media buying, e-sampling, on-ground (in-store) activations, influencer marketing, out-of-home media
- Reporting: goal attainment, ROI tracking, customer engagement, CPC/CPI/CPO and conversion-rate analytics

## API status (verified 2026-08-12)

Quantum publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API reference, no SDK,
no webhook catalog, and no machine-readable contract (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL,
Postman collection) on any host Quantum controls. Contract discovery probed
`/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/v1/openapi.json`, `/api-docs`, `/docs`,
`/redoc`, `/graphql`, `/llms.txt` and the full `/.well-known/*` set against
`quantums.com.sa`, `quantums.sa`, `app.one.quantums.com.sa` and `api.quantums.com.sa`.
Every path returned 404.

The Quantum One tenant application does expose an `/apikeys` route
(https://app.one.quantums.com.sa/apikeys, HTTP 200, renders only after authentication),
so the platform issues API keys to signed-in customers. That surface is customer-only:
its contract is not published, so an agent or integrator cannot evaluate or call it.

Do not infer endpoints, parameters, authentication schemes or rate limits for Quantum from
this file or from any other source. None are published.
